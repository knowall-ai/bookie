= Bank balance history and feed freshness
:toc: left
:icons: font

Each bank account card (Home and *Accounting → Bank accounts*) shows the account's balance over the
last four weeks, as Xero's dashboard does, and says plainly how fresh its figures are. The whole
card, chart included, opens the account's transactions.

Bookie never interpolates or invents a point. A history is built only from figures the source
gives, and where the source can't give one the card says so instead of drawing a chart.

== Where the history comes from

The contract is the optional `BankModule.balanceHistory()` in `web/src/lib/connectors/types.ts`.
It returns every account's history in one read (`BankBalanceHistories`), so a page makes one call
however many cards it shows. The cards stream it in after the balances. The maths is shared in
`web/src/lib/connectors/balance-history.ts`: start from a closing balance the source reports and
walk back a day at a time, taking off each day's movements, summing in whole cents.

[cols="1,4",options="header"]
|===
|Source |History

|Xero
|Daily closing balances reconstructed from the Bank Summary closing balance and the window's bank
transactions and payments, kept only when they walk back exactly to Bank Summary's opening
balance. Otherwise (manual journals to a bank account, more than 1,000 items in four weeks), and
for foreign-currency accounts, weekly points straight from Bank Summary, in base currency as Xero
reports them. See link:XERO_COVERAGE.adoc[Xero API coverage] for the calls.

|Business Central with the Bookie BC extension
|Daily closing balances from the extension's `bankAccountBalances` (Balance, the sum of all the
account's bank ledger entries) and `bankAccountLedgerEntries` posted after the window opens,
future-dated postings taken out of today's balance. In the account's own currency. A ledger
longer than ten pages isn't cut short: the card shows no history instead.

|Business Central without the extension
|None. API v2.0 publishes neither bank account ledger entries nor the G/L account a bank account
posts to, so the card says "Balance history isn't available from Business Central".

|Demo organisations
|Fictional: walked back from each demo account's balance through its demo register.
|===

Histories are cached for five minutes. The cache key starts with the Entra tenant id from the
verified sign-in, then the connection (Xero) or environment and company (BC). A history older than
a minute shows the time it was read ("read at 14:05") so a cached chart never passes for live.

== Freshness

* *Bank feed:* `BankAccount.feedDate` is the date of the latest statement line the bank feed
  brought in, only when the source reports it ("Bank feed: last statement line 9 Oct 2026").
  Neither Xero's Accounting API nor Business Central's says when a feed last ran, so for both the
  card says "Feed status not available", with the reason (`BankModule.feedNote`) as a tooltip.
  The time the page loaded is never shown as a sync time.
* *Last statement:* the statement balance and date, where the source has one. In Business Central
  that's the last statement posted in a bank reconciliation (Bookie BC extension).
* *Read live:* every card ends with "Read live from Xero just now" (or Business Central): Bookie
  stores no accounting data and reads the books on every visit.

== Accessibility

The chart is decorative for assistive technology. Each card carries a sentence instead ("Balance
over the last 4 weeks: £1,000.00 on 20 Sept 2026 to £1,150.00 on 18 Oct 2026, lowest ...,
highest ..."), referenced from the card's link with `aria-describedby`. Hovering the chart shows
the day's date and balance.
