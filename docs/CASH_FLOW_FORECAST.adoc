= Cash flow forecast
KnowAll AI
:toc: left

*Reporting › Cash flow forecast* shows the bank balance week by week (13 weeks) or month by month
(6 or 12 months), in one currency. Every figure belongs to one labelled layer, and the layers are
never mixed:

[cols="1,3", options="header"]
|===
| Layer | From

| Known | Bank balances, and approved unpaid invoices and bills on their due dates
| Drafts (off by default) | Draft and awaiting-approval invoices and bills
| Planned | Items a person typed in, stored in the organisation's SharePoint
| Projection (off by default) | The monthly average of real bank transactions over 3, 6 or 12 months
| Pipeline (off by default) | Open sales opportunities × their chance of success
|===

The chart, table, CSV export and Bookie's `cash_flow_forecast` tool all use the same computed
figures.

== Pipeline (weighted)

Business Central only. Its standard API (v2.0 `opportunities`) gives each opportunity an estimated
value, a chance of success and an estimated closing date. The values are in the company's local
currency, so the pipeline is in the organisation's base currency. Xero has no opportunities, so the
switch is disabled there with the reason. The portal checks for the connector's `opportunities`
module rather than the source.

Switch on *Pipeline (weighted)* (`?pipeline=1`; `include_pipeline` in Bookie's tool). Each open
opportunity adds income of *estimated value × chance of success ÷ 100*, rounded to the penny, on
its estimated closing date. That date is when the deal is expected to close; when the client pays
may differ, and the page says so.

No guessing. An open opportunity is listed under *Not in the pipeline*, with the reason, when it
has:

* no estimated value, or no chance of success (or 0%);
* no estimated closing date (Business Central's `0001-01-01`), or one that has passed;
* a closing date after the forecast's horizon;
* another currency.

Won, lost and closed opportunities are left out: won work is already in the invoices. Bookie reads
up to 5,000 open opportunities and refuses to forecast rather than show part of a larger pipeline.
