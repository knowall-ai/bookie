= Adding a bank account
:toc: left
:icons: font

*Accounting → Bank accounts → Add bank account* creates a bank account in the organisation's own
books, the way Xero's *Add Bank Account* does. Bookie stores nothing: the account exists only in
Xero or Business Central.

== What the form asks

The form asks only what the source system accepts. Each connector declares its rules
(`BankModule.newAccount` in `web/src/lib/connectors/types.ts`) and the button appears only when the
connector has `bank.createAccount`, those rules, and a currency list.

[cols="1,2,2"]
|===
| Field | Xero | Business Central

| Account type | Bank account, Credit card or PayPal (`BankAccountType` `BANK`, `CREDITCARD`, `PAYPAL`) | Not asked: BC bank accounts have no kind
| Account name | Required, up to 150 characters (`Name`) | Required, up to 100 characters (`displayName`)
| Account number | Required (`BankAccountNumber`). Labelled "Last 4 digits of the card" or "PayPal email address" for those kinds, as in Xero | Optional, up to 30 characters (`bankAccountNumber`)
| Code | Optional, up to 10 characters (`Code`) | Optional, up to 20 characters (`number`). Left blank, BC takes the next number from the *Bank Account Nos.* series
| IBAN | Not asked | Optional (`iban`)
| Currency | Required, one of the organisation's currencies (`CurrencyCode`) | Required, the local currency or one from the Currencies list (`currencyCode`, sent blank for the local currency)
|===

=== Currency

The currency picker lists only the currencies the books actually have: Xero's `GET Currencies`, or
Business Central's local currency plus its Currencies list. Nothing is preselected and nothing is
inferred from the country. If the currency isn't there, the form says where to add it (Xero:
organisation settings, Currencies; Business Central: the Currencies page, linked). The server action
re-reads the currency list before writing, so a tampered request can't use a currency the books don't
have.

== How it writes

* *Xero:* `PUT Accounts` with `Type: BANK` (scope `accounting.settings`, already used for "Add
  account" on the chart of accounts).
* *Business Central:* API v2.0 `POST bankAccounts`. Microsoft documents GET, POST, PATCH and DELETE
  for the `bankAccount` entity, with `number`, `displayName`, `bankAccountNumber`, `iban`,
  `currencyCode`, `blocked` and `intercompanyEnabled` writable. The API page doesn't expose the
  *Bank Account Posting Group*, so the form says plainly that it must be set on the new account in
  Business Central before anything is posted to it, and links to the Bank Accounts page.
* *Demo books:* a fictional, in-memory account (`BOOKIE_DEV_AUTH=demo` only) with the same rules as
  Xero, so the Playwright tests can exercise the flow.

Like every write to live books, the action (`createBankAccount` in
`web/src/lib/portal/actions/bank.ts`) validates with zod, re-resolves the organisation with
`orgContext()`, and runs only after the person confirms in a `ConfirmDialog`. Errors from Xero or
Business Central are shown as the source system worded them.
